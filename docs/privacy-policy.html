Privacy Policy for Merge Mess

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Merge Mess ("we", "our", or "the game") is an offline-friendly casual merge puzzle game. This Privacy Policy informs players about how personal data is handled.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

Merge Mess does not require registration, login accounts, email addresses, names, or phone numbers. We do not maintain a user database or store personal data on external servers.

2. Device Local Storage

The game saves your progress locally on your device (browser LocalStorage or device preferences):

  • High score & game progress
  • Coins earned from customer orders
  • Unlocked themes (Classic Food, Tamil Alphabet)
  • Daily challenge streaks
  • Audio settings

This data stays on your personal device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser/app storage.

3. Advertisements

When published on the Google Play Store, the app may use Google AdMob to display optional rewarded video ads (such as for undoing a move or continuing after game over). AdMob may use device identifiers and cookies to serve ads in compliance with Google's policies and applicable privacy regulations.

4. Donations and Payments

Any voluntary developer donations via UPI are executed directly between your preferred UPI application and the developer's UPI ID. Merge Mess does not process, store, or receive payment card data or banking credentials.

5. Children's Privacy

Merge Mess is designed for casual players of all ages, including children learning Tamil letters. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.
